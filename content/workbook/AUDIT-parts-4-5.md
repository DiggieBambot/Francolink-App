# Audit · Parts 4–5 against CEFR B1/B2

Date: 2026-08-30 · Resolves PRD open question #4

## Verdict

The book is honestly **A0 → B1-minus**, with a B1/B2 *preview*. What is there is
well taught; there is simply not enough of it, and one section depends on a
tense the book never defines.

### The proportion problem

| Part | Level claimed | Words | Exercises |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | A0–A2 | ~9,260 | 25 |
| 3 | A2–B1 | 1,353 | ~2 |
| 4 | past tenses | 1,346 | 2 |
| 5 | B1–B2 | ~1,400 (excl. back matter) | 3 |

The half of the book that carries the harder 60% of the syllabus is **23% of
the words and 5 of 30 exercises**. Practice volume is inverted: learners need
*more* drilling at B1/B2, not less.

## Defects (must fix — these are wrong, not just thin)

1. **§5.2 uses the plus-que-parfait, which the book never teaches.** The third
   si-pattern is given as "Si + PLUS-QUE-PARFAIT → conditionnel passé" and the
   dialogue uses *si j'avais su*. The tense has no section, no table, no
   exercise. A reader hits an undefined term in the book's hardest section.
   → Fixed by new §4.6.

2. **§4.3 states the avoir-agreement rule too absolutely.** "With avoir there
   is normally NO agreement" is a useful A2 lie that becomes a B1 error: *les
   livres que j'ai **lus***. The book will have to unteach itself.
   → Fixed by new §4.7.

3. **§4.3's être-verb list is incomplete.** Missing *devenir, revenir,
   rentrer, passer*, and the B1 point that *monter/descendre/sortir/passer/
   rentrer* switch to **avoir** when they take a direct object (*j'ai sorti la
   poubelle*).

4. **§5.1 Exercise 28.3 contains its own answer** — "(pleuvoir → pleuvra)".
   Sloppy; delete the gloss.

5. **§5.1 "stem appeller-"** is confusingly written. The rule is that *appeler*
   doubles the l before a silent ending: *appell-* → *j'appellerai*.

6. **Le conditionnel passé** gets three bullets and no conjugation table, in a
   section that then builds an entire si-pattern on it.

## Missing for B1 (absent from the entire book, not just Part 5)

- **Object pronouns** — *le/la/les, lui/leur, y, en*. The largest single gap in
  the book. B1 speech is impossible without them; the manuscript has none.
- **Relative pronouns** — *qui, que, où, dont*.
- **Comparative and superlative** — *plus/moins/aussi… que, meilleur, le plus*.
  This is A2 and it is missing.
- **The imperative** — absent entirely. Also A2.
- **Adverb formation** (-ment) and placement.
- **Depuis / il y a / pendant** — the classic B1 time-expression trap.
- **Plus-que-parfait** (see defect 1).

## Missing for B2

Part 5's own introduction promises "nuance and opinion" and then delivers three
tenses. B2 is largely about *discourse*, and none of it is present:

- **Subjunctive after conjunctions** — *bien que, pour que, avant que, jusqu'à
  ce que, à condition que*. Only the *que*-trigger families are taught.
- **Le subjonctif passé** — *que j'aie fait*.
- **Reported speech and the concordance des temps** — core B2.
- **Logical connectors** — *cependant, néanmoins, en revanche, d'ailleurs,
  or, ainsi*. This is what "nuance and opinion" actually means.
- **The passive voice**, and how French avoids it with *on* and *se*.
- **Le gérondif** — *en + -ant*.
- **Le futur antérieur**.
- **Register** beyond tu/vous.

## Recommendation

Two routes:

**(a) Soften the claim** to "A0 → B1" and reprice at $17. Cheap, honest, and
loses the strongest thing about the offer.

**(b) Write the missing content.** ~5,500 words closes the credible gap to B2,
raises the book from ~70 to ~95 pages, and supports the $27 price with room to
test $37. Since the book is customer-acquisition rather than a revenue line,
the extra perceived value is worth more than the writing time costs.

**Recommend (b).** Draft is in `PART-6-B1-B2-expansion.md`: nine new sections
in the book's existing house style, exercises 31–45, with answer key.
