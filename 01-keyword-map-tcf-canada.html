Why this one
The hub's primary is 'tcf canada exam' rather than 'tcf canada' (18,100). The head term is navigational: page one is test centres registering candidates (af.ca, Alliance Française Vancouver and Edmonton) under an AI Overview built from France Éducation International. 'tcf canada exam' and 'tcf canada test' (2,900) carry the explainer intent the hub actually serves. Keep 'TCF Canada' at the start of the title so the head term is still matched.
Use this as the H1 subject and put it in the first 100 words. Each URL has its own primary (one per cluster), because this map covers five existing pages and one proposed page, not a single page. The part pages are small in English: writing about 1,100/month across all variants, speaking about 900, listening about 700 (most of it practice intent), reading about 400. The score cluster is about 3,200/month on its own.
