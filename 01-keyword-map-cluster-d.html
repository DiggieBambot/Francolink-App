Why this one
The weakest competition in any FrancoLink keyword measured so far, grammar included. Competition 0.01, and the pages that rank average 0.2 referring domains, meaning essentially nobody with authority is contesting it. Page one is Reddit at position 1, a Facebook group at 3, Quora at 5 and YouTube at 7. Four of the eight results are user-generated. A credentialed, structured answer has an unusually clear run.
Use this as the H1 subject and put it in the first 100 words. Two cautions. The AI volume is volatile: 152 to 199 a month through late 2025, then a spike to 1,828 in May 2026 and back to 294 in August, so treat 294 as the current reading and not a trend. And the honest answer to this question is not 'no', which is what every thin page says. The FSI puts French in Category I at 600 to 750 hours, which is both encouraging and a real number; cite FSI directly rather than a blog repeating it.

03

Fan-out questions to answer

answer capture

These are the follow-up queries AI engines expand your topic into. Each one becomes an H2 with a two-sentence answer directly underneath.

  1. How long does it take to learn French?People Also Ask, and the highest-volume secondary in the map at 1,600/mo. The FSI Category I figure is the answer, cited to FSI directly rather than to a blog repeating it.
  2. What is the 80/20 rule in French?People Also Ask. Notable: this same question also appeared in the 'french grammar' PAA block on 9 September. It is the one question both clusters share, and nobody on either page answers it properly.
  3. On a scale of 1 to 10, how hard is it to learn French?Related searches, and it recurs across four of the difficulty keywords. People want a number. Give them one and justify it.
  4. Is French harder than Spanish?Related searches plus its own keyword at 320/mo. The honest answer is split: pronunciation harder, grammar comparable, and saying so beats picking a side.
  5. Which is harder, French or English?People Also Ask. Answer from the English speaker's position, which is the audience the brief names.
  6. Is French hard to learn for beginners?Related searches. Distinct from the head term because it is about the A1 stage specifically, which maps to your own curriculum.
  7. What makes French pronunciation hard?Google's AI Overview built a section from this: silent letters, nasal vowels, liaisons. Cover it or the page cannot be cited for the 'why it is hard' half.
  8. How much French vocabulary overlaps with English?The AI Overview quotes 30 to 40 percent. Verify against a primary source before repeating it; the Overview attributes it to a competitor blog, not to research.
  9. Does noun gender make French harder?AI Overview section two. Links naturally to Cluster A's grammar posts, which is how the two clusters join up.
  10. What is the #1 easiest language to learn?People Also Ask, and it connects to the 8,100/mo support keyword. Answer it honestly even though the answer is usually not French, then say where French actually sits.
