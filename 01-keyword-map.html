Why this one
It is the subject of the page, and the intent matches. 'french conjugation' carries 8,100 a month and 1,780 in LLMs, six times the volume, but DataForSEO resolves its core keyword to 'french verb conjugator': those people want a tool, not a lesson, and pointing a teaching hub at them is the classic volume-over-intent mistake.
Use this as the H1 subject and put it in the first 100 words. Do not chase the conjugator traffic here. The hub wins by explaining, and the workbook is the thing it explains from. Note the seasonality: this cluster peaks September to May and halves over the summer, so publish before term starts.

Fan-out questions to answer

answer capture

These are the follow-up queries AI engines expand your topic into. Each one becomes an H2 with a two-sentence answer directly underneath.

  1. What are the basic grammar rules in French?People Also Ask, verbatim. This is the hub's opening answer: give the rules as a numbered list in the first 100 words.
  2. What is the 80/20 rule in French?People Also Ask. Nobody on page one answers it properly. The workbook's 45 rules are literally this answer.
  3. Is it "j'ai besoin" or "je besoin"?People Also Ask. A single worked example that proves the site explains the reason, not just the form.
  4. What does "est-ce que" mean literally?People Also Ask. Answer-first, two sentences, then link to the question-formation post.
  5. Can I say "je suis bien"?People Also Ask, nested under est-ce que. Same treatment: the answer, then why English speakers get it wrong.
  6. How does French gender and noun agreement work?Google's AI Overview built its first section from this. Cover it or the hub cannot be cited.
  7. How do French articles and adjectives agree with the noun?AI Overview section two. Include the BAGS adjective ordering, which the Overview quotes.
  8. How does French verb conjugation work?AI Overview section three, and the highest-AI-volume topic in the map. Summarise -er/-ir/-re here, then hand off.
  9. What order should I learn French grammar in?Related searches show 'French grammar A1'. Answer with the A0-B2 progression. This is where the workbook is the natural next step.
  10. Is there a French grammar PDF I can download?Related searches: 'French grammar pdf', 'All French Grammar rules PDF'. Real demand, and the workbook ships as a PDF.
